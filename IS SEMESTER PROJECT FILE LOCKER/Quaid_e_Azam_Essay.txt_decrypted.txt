Early Life
Born: 25th December 1876, Karachi (then part of British India).

Family: Son of Jinnah Poonja, a merchant, and Mithibai.

Education: Studied at Sindh Madrasatul Islam and later at Christian Missionary School. He went to London and enrolled at Lincoln’s Inn to study law, becoming one of the youngest barristers of his time.

Principles and Vision
Quaid-e-Azam gave Pakistan three guiding principles: Unity, Faith, and Discipline.

He envisioned Pakistan as a country where justice, equality, and freedom of religion would prevail.

His honesty and integrity earned him respect even from opponents; Mahatma Gandhi once called him “an impossible man” due to his firm principles.

Death and Legacy
Jinnah passed away on 11th September 1948 in Karachi, just a year after Pakistan’s independence.

Despite his short tenure, his vision laid the foundation of the new nation.

His birthday, 25th December, is celebrated annually as a national holiday in Pakistan.
